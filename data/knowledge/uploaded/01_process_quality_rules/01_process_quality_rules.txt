APMOA Process Quality Rules

Fitness measures how well a discovered process model reproduces observed behavior.
Precision measures unnecessary behavior permitted by the model.
Generalization measures the ability to represent valid behavior not directly observed.
Simplicity measures structural complexity.

Prototype quality thresholds:
fitness minimum = 0.80
precision minimum = 0.50
generalization minimum = 0.70
simplicity minimum = 0.40

Any quality violation should trigger the autonomous rediscovery policy.
If quality passes, the model may be retained unless the reasoning layer explicitly requests rediscovery.
