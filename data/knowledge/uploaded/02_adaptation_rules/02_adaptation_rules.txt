APMOA Autonomous Adaptation Rules

If precision is below its minimum threshold, use a precision-oriented rediscovery strategy.
If fitness is below its minimum threshold, use a fitness-oriented rediscovery strategy.
If generalization is below its minimum threshold, use a balanced or generalization-oriented strategy.
If simplicity is below its minimum threshold, consider a simplicity-oriented strategy.

Available discovery algorithms:
Inductive Miner
Alpha Miner
Alpha+ Miner
Heuristics Miner
Split Miner

A safe default is KEEP when quality thresholds pass and no explicit rediscovery request is produced.
