Process Model Evaluation Metrics

Fitness measures how well the discovered process model can reproduce the behavior observed in the event log.

Precision measures how much unnecessary behavior the discovered model allows beyond the observed behavior.

Generalization measures how well the model can represent behavior that was not directly observed in the available sample.

Simplicity measures the structural complexity of the discovered process model.

A balanced process model should consider fitness, precision, generalization, and simplicity rather than optimizing a single metric.
